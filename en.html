Macro Hedge Trading Challenge

A replication of Elm Wealth's “Crystal Ball Trading Challenge” (Haghani, White & Bell, 2023) · the four-asset edition

How it works

  • You will see 15 “tomorrow's front pages” — the newspaper front page from the day after a major macro event (the date is hidden, and the price moves for the four assets have been blacked out, as in the original experiment).
  • You are seeing the news a day early: you enter at the last close before the news breaks, and are force-closed at the first close after the news has been absorbed.
  • Four tradable assets: SSE Composite, S&P 500, gold (COMEX futures), Bitcoin. Go long (bet on a rise) or short (bet on a fall).
  • Use the sliders to set the position multiple (leverage) for each asset — up to 3× per asset, and no more than 6× in total (the sum of the absolute values). 1× = a position equal to your entire capital.
  • Starting capital $1,000,000 (virtual). No trading costs or financing fees. If your equity hits zero you are wiped out.
  • You can skip any round you are unsure about. Every event really happened, and every return comes from real historical prices (sources in the footer).
In the original experiment, 118 finance graduate students were handed the same crystal ball: about half of them lost money, one in six was wiped out, and the average return was just +3.2%. Five veteran macro traders averaged +130%. Can you do better?
